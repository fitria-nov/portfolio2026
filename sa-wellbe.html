Well-Be Application

A comprehensive healthcare monitoring application designed for Alzheimer's and Dementia patients, featuring real-time GPS tracking and timeline memories.

Read Full SRS PDF

Project Overview

The Well-Be App serves as a proactive technology innovation to monitor elderly patients experiencing dementia and memory loss. The system acts as a bridge between the Patient, Caregiver, Family, and Admin, specifically utilizing Smartwatch integration.

My Role: System Analyst

As part of the analysis team, I was specifically responsible for formulating the User Interface (User Stories & Scenarios), establishing the General & Design Constraints, and mapping out the Assumptions and Dependencies.

User Stories & Persona Analysis

Defined specific requirements from the perspective of four primary actors to ensure accurate functionality alignment.

👤 Patient

The primary receiver of care who requires safety monitoring.

  • Wants to save and recall precious memories.
  • Requires real-time location monitoring for safety.

🧑‍⚕️ Caregiver

Responsible for direct physical and mental monitoring.

  • Needs to monitor health conditions to provide appropriate care.
  • Requires real-time location notifications.

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Family

External supervisor wanting to remain involved in the patient's life.

  • Wants remote access to the patient's location.
  • Wants to view and manage the patient's timeline memories.

🛡️ Admin

System manager maintaining data integrity.

  • Manages caregiver scheduling and data.
  • Generates monthly health progress reports.

Constraints & Dependencies

Mapped out critical boundaries for the architecture to ensure system reliability and legal compliance.

  • Regulatory Policies: The app handles highly sensitive medical data and must comply with strict privacy regulations (e.g., GDPR, HIPAA).
  • Hardware Limitations: Requires seamless integration with Smartwatch GPS modules while optimizing for battery consumption.
  • Security: All user data is secured with AES-256 encryption for both storage and transmission, reinforced by Two-Factor Authentication.
  • Dependencies: Relies on third-party GPS APIs, OS updates (iOS/Android), and constant server uptime for real-time tracking.
